\section{Conclusion}

In this work we have found the domain of the phase space where the balance between the Heisenberg interaction, the DM interaction and the applied magnetic field is such that skyrmions form.
This domain qualitatively matches previous literature \cite{10.1063/9.0000082}.
Besides the skyrmion regions we also find the spiral phase and the ferromagnetic phase.

When quenching the system to higher temperatures the number of skyrmions that form decreases. 
The same is found when increasing the temperature after skyrmions have formed.
The critical temperature is higher for the destruction of skyrmions than for the formation of skyrmions, suggesting that the claim that skyrmions are protected by topology is true.
\\
In future work the algorithm could be refined to solve the mismatch between the number of detected skyrmions and global skyrmions number.
Another interesting avenue for future work could be to classify the spiral phase instead of identifying it as the absence of the ferromagnetic and skyrmion phase using the static structure factor \cite{Haller_2022}.
