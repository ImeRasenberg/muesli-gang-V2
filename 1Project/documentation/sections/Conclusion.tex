\section{Conclusion}
\textbf{We still need to add the goal in the introduction}

From our simulations we find the domain where the balance between the Heisenberg interaction, the DM interaction and the applied magnetic field is is such that skyrmions can exist. We also find the spiral phase and the paramagnetic phase.

We also find that with rising temperature the skyrmion formation decreases. more over with increasing temperature the stability of the skyrmions is also found to decrease. However these two dependence are different where they stop forming at a lower temperature than that they are destroyed, suggesting that the claim that skyrmions are protected by topology is true.
